If you need support now

VirWave is for relaxing and resetting. It is not medical care or a crisis service. If you’re struggling, you don’t have to wait:

  • UK: call Samaritans on 116 123, free, day or night.
  • US: call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline).
  • If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number.

If you feel lightheaded while breathing, stop and breathe normally.
