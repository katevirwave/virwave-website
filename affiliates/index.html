How it works

  1. Apply. Tell us where you post and who you reach. We review every application.
  2. Get your code. Once approved, you get a creator code and two links:
    • App link: virwave.com/ref/YOURCODE opens the App Store or Google Play and credits you.
    • Breathe in Claude: virwave.com/breathe/connect?ref=YOURCODE shows people how to add VirWave Breathe to Claude, with your code built in.
  3. Share it your way. Posts, stories, videos, newsletters, talks. A referral counts when someone subscribes within 30 days of using your link.
  4. Get paid monthly by PayPal or Wise, after a 30-day hold. US creators are paid in USD; everyone else in GBP or USD.

Who it’s for

Creators anywhere in the world aged 18 or over, whose content is for a general audience. (We can’t pay creators in countries under UK or US sanctions.) Wellbeing, productivity, neurodiversity, fitness, study and founder audiences tend to fit well. VirWave is for people aged 13 and over, so please don’t promote it as an app for children.

Two simple rules

  • Disclose every post. Label it clearly, for example “#ad” at the start, and use the platform’s paid-partnership label where there is one.
