Kids and grown-ups start equal

At your first class, everyone hatches as a Seedling: a three-year-old, a nine-year-old, and the mum, dad, or grandparent next to them. There are no levels and no badges. We travel the VirWave lands together, each at our own pace.

  • The same shape every weekEvery class follows the same calm pattern, so children know what comes next.
  • Gentle on the sensesSoft music, calm lighting, and a quiet spot to rest whenever someone needs it.
  • A seated version of every poseEveryone can take part, from the floor or from a chair.
  • Joining in looks different for everyoneMoving, watching, and resting all count.

Each term is a journey through the four lands

A term is 10 weeks. Each land brings a new breath and new poses, and every class practises the land at its own level.

  1. Weeks 1–2

    Sukadam · Rooted

    Home, where everyone feels safe. We find our feet and our breath.

    Mountain, tree, and child’s pose

  2. Weeks 3–4

    Opowatha · Naming

    The land of rivers and reflection. We ask each other, “What colour are you?”

    Cat and cow

  3. Weeks 5–6

    Kridasar · Reaching

    The land of joy and play. We stretch, reach, and wiggle.

    Downward dog and happy baby

  4. Weeks 7–8

    Kithkin · Brave

    The land of courage. We feel strong and find our brave.

    Plank and upward dog

  5. Weeks 9–10

    All four lands

    We travel the whole map, and grown-ups join the last class to celebrate.

    Lotus, plus everyone’s favourites

What a class looks like

  1. Arrive

    We settle in and ask, “What colour are you today?” Every colour is welcome.

  2. Breathe

    We trace a shape with a finger and breathe along with it.

  3. Move

    Poses and games from this week’s land.

  4. Rest

    We lie down, slow our breathing, and check in again: “What colour are you now?”

Questions

Do grown-ups join in?

In family classes, yes. That’s the point: everyone starts as a Seedling. For ages 3–5, a grown-up stays in the room. For ages 6–9, grown-ups can drop off.

Does my child need to have done yoga before?

No. Everyone starts at the beginning.

What if my child doesn’t want to join in?

That’s okay. They can watch, rest in the quiet spot, or join in when they’re ready. Every pose also has a seated version.

What should we bring?

Comfy clothes and a water bottle. We do yoga in bare feet or grippy socks.

Is it safe?

Yes. Before classes start, every VirWave Yoga teacher is DBS-checked and insured, and every class follows a written safeguarding policy.

Is this therapy?

No. VirWave Yoga is movement, breathing, and play for everyday wellbeing. It isn’t medical care or therapy.
