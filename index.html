Why VirWave exists

Regulation isn’t a skill you’re failing at.

Kate Julia is an engineer and a registered yoga teacher trained in trauma-informed practice. She builds VirWave herself, writing production code on the live app every week.

Her TEDxUCL talk, The Myth of CALM, argues that calm isn’t something you earn alone. It’s something we build for each other. VirWave is her way of building it.

Try it in Claude

A breathing break, right in the chat.
