What we're building

The app is the wedge. The universe is the moat.

The live product is the adult wellness app. Alongside it: a transmedia IP world and physical companions that all tie back to the same portrait. One universe, three surfaces, compounding.

Reflect + Connect. Twelve breathing patterns, a four-layer emotional portrait, and seventy-eight relational pairings that give you language for how to show up.
