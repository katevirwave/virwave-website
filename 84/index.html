What colour are you?

For 84 days, ask yourself one question each day: what colour are you? Then take one breath. That’s the whole challenge.

There are no streaks and no guilt. If you miss a day, the next one is still there. Dark colours count too. There’s no bad colour, only the one you are today.

Why 84 days?

A UCL study followed people for 84 days while they built a new daily habit. On average it took 66 days for the habit to feel automatic, though it varied a lot from person to person. Missing the odd day didn’t undo anyone’s progress. So the challenge runs for the full 84 days, with check-in points along the way.

Study: Lally, P., van Jaarsveld, C. H. M., Potts, H. W. W. & Wardle, J. (2010). How are habits formed: Modelling habit formation in the real world. European Journal of Social Psychology, 40(6), 998–1009.
