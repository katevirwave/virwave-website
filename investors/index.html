What we're building

One practice, two places to use it.

The breathing app on your phone, and VirWave Breathe inside Claude. Same shapes, same pace, same no-guilt approach, wherever you are when you need it.

The app

Twelve breathing patterns, drawn as shapes you can follow with your eyes. Next on the roadmap: an emotional portrait and relational pairings that give you language for how to show up for the people you love.

  • Live · iOS + Android
  • Twelve breathing patterns
  • Portrait & pairings · in development

VirWave Breathe

A breathing break inside Claude. Ask for one and Claude draws a calm, animated session right in the chat, hold-free unless you ask otherwise.

  • Live · free for Claude
  • Same patterns as the app
  • More AI assistants · next
