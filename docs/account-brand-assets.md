# Account sign-in artwork

Use the existing `assets/icon.png` from VirWave v3 for the VirWave identity.

- Google: `assets/img/partners/google-sign-in.png`, downloaded unchanged from `https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/g-logo.png`, linked by the [official branding guide](https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines). Preserve its colors and aspect ratio on the white provider button.
- Apple: `assets/img/partners/apple-sign-in.svg`, copied unchanged from `Sign in with Apple - Left Aligned/SVG/Logo - SIWA - Left-aligned - Black - Medium.svg` in Apple's [official artwork pack](https://devimages-cdn.apple.com/design/resources/download/Logo-Sign-in-with-Apple.dmg). Sebastian explicitly authorized accepting the artwork licence on 25 September 2026 for this sign-in button. Retain the artwork's internal padding; display it at full button height, without cropping or tinting. Use only for the enabled Sign in with Apple interface under Apple's licence and [design guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sign-in-with-apple).

These marks are not VirWave-owned artwork and are not general-purpose marketing graphics. They are served locally so the account page needs no third-party asset or script access.
