How connecting will work

  1. In Claude, open Settings → Connectors. Choose to add a custom connector if your account has that option.
  2. Use the VirWave server address below. Preview testers may also need the client settings supplied with their invitation.

Connecting a custom assistant?

This is VirWave’s account-connected MCP server address. Your assistant must support remote connections and VirWave sign-in. An address alone does not complete setup.

https://virwave.com/mcp

Preview access is still being tested. If your assistant asks for a client ID you haven’t been given, setup is not available for your account yet.

Once you’re connected

Try a simple request:

“Start a two-minute breathing break with VirWave.”

When the breathing player appears, press Begin to start. Account-connected sessions can be saved to your VirWave practice history.

You’re in control

Review the permissions before connecting. You can remove an assistant’s access from your VirWave account. Your app access and saved sessions stay.
