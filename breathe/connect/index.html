Add VirWave Breathe to Claude

Free · No account · No personal data stored

Ask for a breathing break and a calm, animated VirWave session appears right in your chat. It waits for you to press Begin, paces your breath, and works on the web, the desktop app, and your phone.

Your connector address

https://xswebtvkueusdaeboizp.supabase.co/functions/v1/breathe

Add it in one minute

  1. Open Claude (opens in new tab) on the web or in the desktop app, and go to Customize → Connectors.
  2. Select +, then Add custom connector.
  3. Name it VirWave Breathe, paste the address above, and select Add. Leave the OAuth settings empty.
  4. In any chat, say “Let’s breathe” or “I need a breathing break”. The first time, Claude asks you to connect or allow VirWave Breathe.

Once it’s added, it’s there on your phone too: open the Claude app and ask for a breathing break.

On Claude’s free plan you can add one custom connector. Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise can add more.

Your privacy

VirWave Breathe needs no account and stores no personal data. We count anonymous usage, such as which assistant connected and which session ran, and never your IP address, device, identity, or how you said you feel. Read the privacy policy.

Sessions are for relaxing and resetting. They are not medical care. If you feel lightheaded, stop and breathe normally.
